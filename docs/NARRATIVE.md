# Narrative bible (v0.1)

## Opening

2053. The grid died in 2047 — The Great Collapse. Cities went dark in three days. Governments followed the power. What remains are settlements that trade fuel, medicine, and ammunition like oxygen.

You do not play a chosen one. You play a crew that either keeps a settlement supplied or keeps the roads “legal.”

## Cinematic (script, not produced)

Black. Radio hiss. A Sentinel dispatcher: “Unauthorized heavy, Dust Valley mile 19.” Cut to a Raptor with no plates. MEDIC counts magazines. TRACE marks the dust plume. Title: OUTRUN: AFTERMATH.

## Map stories

- **Dead City** — financial district that burned from the inside; Sentinels tax the remaining wells.
- **Dust Valley** — the only fuel artery west; convoys vanish in haboobs.
- **Blackwood Forest** — jamming canopy; SHADE’s people move here.
- **Frostbite Ridge** — hydro plant; both sides need the turbines.
- **Ironworks** — pre-collapse arms foundry; CIRCUIT wants the CNC intact, TORQUE wants it looted.
- **Hollow Creek** — farms that pay Sentinel protection; Rogues smuggle grain out.
- **Ashfall** — town under volcanic ash from a cracked geo plant; extraction is always a sprint.

## Seasonal hook

Season 1 “Blackout Protocol”: someone is selling working satellites. Neither faction should own them unchallenged.

## Post-match summary (template)

Director fills: map, type, MVP, whether the extract vehicle left, who was captured. Tone is after-action, not comic.
