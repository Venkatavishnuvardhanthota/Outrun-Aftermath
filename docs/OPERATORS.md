# Operators — biographies and kits

Balance numbers live in `OperatorCatalog` / `AbilityCatalog`. This file is fiction + design intent.

## Rogues

**REX** — Ex-Sentinel NCO who deserted after a checkpoint massacre. Assault. Wants enough fuel to disappear, not to free anyone.

**NOVA** — Settlement scout, younger than the war. Sees too much; sells maps to both sides when hungry.

**MEDIC** — Field surgeon who charges in blood and bullets. Will drop a stim on a Sentinel if the contract says so.

**TORQUE** — Garage mystic. Loves machines more than people. Builds extract rigs from scrap.

**SHADE** — Smuggler. Quiet. Owes a debt in Ashfall. Ghost kit is stolen Sentinel recon tech.

## Sentinels

**TITAN** — Believes order is kindness. Has executed looters. Sleeps badly.

**TRACE** — Lost a convoy to SHADE. Hunt is personal; procedure is a costume.

**VIPER** — Black-site graduate. Enjoys the work. Command pretends not to.

**CIRCUIT** — Civilian engineer drafted. Wants the foundry running for the settlement, not the flag.

**FALCON** — Interceptor ace. Addicted to speed. Reckless with crews.

Starter unlock: all ten. Cosmetics later.
